About Alyssa

A dietitian, recipe creator, and nutrition communicator.

Alyssa’s interest in nutrition grew from personal health experiences. She earned BS and MS degrees in Nutrition and Food Science at Montclair State University, completed her dietetic internship at Hunter College, and minored in Journalism.
